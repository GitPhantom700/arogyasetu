1. Executive Overview & Problem Context

STRATEGIC CONTEXT

The Rural Western Ghats Healthcare Supply Paradox

In the rugged, high-rainfall mountainous terrain of the Sahyadri range (Pune and Satara districts), primary health clinics face acute, life-threatening stockouts of cold-chain emergency supplies. During the annual monsoon season (June–September), landslides and torrential flash floods frequently sever arterial mountain passes, transforming remote rural clinics into isolated islands.

When a villager in an isolated sub-centre like Velhe or Mahabaleshwar Forest Fringe suffers a high-grade envenomation from a Russell’s Viper or Saw-scaled Viper, survival is strictly governed by the Golden Hours (≤ 2 hours). Conventional public health replenishment protocols depend on centralized district medical stores located in Pune City or Satara town, which require 48 to 96 hours to mobilize shipments through official paperwork channels and flooded roads. Consequently, preventable fatalities occur while clinics wait for deliveries.

PranaVahini dynamically predicts stock exhaustion, discovers optimal peer donor clinics, ensures cold-chain integrity (+2°C to +8°C), and legally authorizes inter-PHC emergency transfers with cryptographic chain-of-custody.

7. AISafetyGuard & The 6 Physical Invariants

ZERO-HALLUCINATION FIREWALL

To guarantee zero patient harm and eliminate algorithmic hallucinations, all Google Gemini outputs are strictly treated as untrusted recommendations and intercepted by the deterministic AISafetyGuard firewall.

Invariant 1: Non-Cannibalization / Zero Donor Starvation CRITICAL SAFETY
Retained_Donor_Stock ≥ max( min_safety_stock, ⌈ 14 × DAC ⌉ )  |  ⌈ 21 × DAC ⌉ (Monsoon Mode)

A donor clinic can never transfer stock if doing so degrades its own inventory below 14 days of Daily Average Consumption (DAC). In monsoon conditions (June–September) across mountain terrain, this buffer is automatically scaled by 1.5× to 21 days. Eliminates the hazard of "cannibalizing" one clinic to save another.

Invariant 2: Physical Inventory Bounding & Non-Negative Clamping CRITICAL SAFETY
0 ≤ Allocated_Quantity ≤ min( Recipient_Deficit, Actual_Physical_Surplus )

Clamps recommended transfer quantities strictly to physically verified on-hand stock batches. Eliminates phantom inventory hallucinations where generative models suggest transferring more units than exist in the donor's physical storage.

Invariant 3: Cold-Chain Verification Gate (+2°C to +8°C) CRITICAL SAFETY
Requires_Cold_Chain(Med) = True ⟹ Donor_ILR_Status = VERIFIED ∧ Recipient_ILR_Status = VERIFIED

Rejects transfer of biologicals (Anti-Snake Venom, Anti-Rabies Vaccines, Insulin, Oxytocin) if either facility lacks verified Ice-Lined Refrigerators (ILR) or continuous cold-chain carrier equipment. Sub-Centres without cold chain (e.g. SC Velhe) cannot act as donor repositories.

Invariant 4: Transit-Aware FEFO Shelf-Life Buffer CLINICAL EFFICACY
Batch_Expiry ≥ Today + ⌈ Transit_Hours / 24 ⌉ + min( 180, ⌈ Deficit / Recipient_DAC ⌉ ) + 7 days

Selected donor batches must not only survive road transit without expiring, but must retain sufficient shelf-life for the recipient clinic to consume the stock before expiration, plus a 7-day administrative safety margin.

Invariant 5: Rural Blackout Baseline Demand Floor RESILIENCE GUARD
Historical_7d_Consumption = 0 ∧ Is_Emergency(Med) = True ⟹ Evaluated_DAC = 1.0 unit/day

When prolonged rural grid outages or cellular blackouts zero out consumption logs, the algorithm prevents calculated DAC from collapsing to 0.0 (which would otherwise cause infinite Days of Inventory calculations and conceal critical stockouts).

Invariant 6: Untrusted Payload Distrust & Prompt Injection Sanitization SECURITY GUARD
Sanitize(Input) ⟹ Strip Control Chars ∧ Reject Override Directives ("retention_buffer: 0")

Strips adversarial prompt injections and malformed JSON payloads. If an adversarial prompt instructs the model to bypass retention quotas, the deterministic validator strictly ignores the model override and re-enforces statutory safety floors.

High-Concurrency Circuit Breaker Architecture

The AI subsystem is protected by an industrial-grade, thread-safe CircuitBreaker:

CLOSED State (Nominal)

Gemini AI handles rebalancing requests. Failures are tracked. Transitions to OPEN upon 3 consecutive timeouts or network errors.

OPEN State (Tripped)

All AI calls are intercepted immediately without network delay (< 2ms). Requests divert to the local deterministic rule engine.

HALF_OPEN State (Probe)

After 60s cooldown, a single worker probe tests API health via semaphore. Includes a 15s hung-worker watchdog timer.

1. Dynamic Burn Rate Engine & Days of Inventory Remaining (DIR)

Module: backend/burn_rate.py

Continuous, rolling algorithmic forecaster that computes Daily Average Consumption (DAC) over a trailing 7-day sliding window, calculates Days of Inventory Remaining (DIR), and flags acute velocity acceleration ($V_{\text{surge}}$) before absolute stockout events occur.

🏥 Concrete Operational Example (Primary Health Centre Paud):
  • Clinical Context: Primary Health Centre Paud (PHC-PUN-02) serves high-elevation paddy farming villages in the Mulshi valley of Pune's Western Ghats.
  • Medication & Stock: Polyvalent Anti-Snake Venom (MED-ASV-01). On-hand inventory is 6 vials across 2 batches. The facility's statutory minimum safety stock floor is 20 vials.
  • Consumption Velocity: Due to a cluster of agricultural Russell's Viper envenomations during monsoon tilling, Paud logged 21 dispensed vials over the preceding 7 days ($\text{DAC} = 21 / 7 = 3.0\text{ vials/day}$).
  • Mathematical Evaluation: $\text{DIR} = \frac{\text{Current Stock}}{\text{DAC}} = \frac{6}{3.0} = 2.0\text{ days}$.
  • System Action: Because $\text{DIR} \le 2.0$, the engine immediately changes Paud's clinical triage status to CRITICAL (Red map marker). It broadcasts an automated SSE event to all connected district dashboards within 40 milliseconds.
🛡️ Safety Invariant: Imposes a baseline floor ($\text{DAC} = 1.0\text{ unit/day}$) if grid outages zero out telemetry. ⚡ Performance: Sub-5ms query using composite index idx_tx_covering.
