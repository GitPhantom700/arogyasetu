Architecture Clarification: Unified Single URL vs Development Server
Why were two ports running during development? Port 5173 was the Vite Hot-Module-Replacement (HMR) bundler used for fast developer iteration, while Port 8000 is the production FastAPI server. In PranaVahini, the backend mounts the compiled production frontend directly at http://localhost:8000/.
Unified Status: In production and single-port deployment, only http://localhost:8000 is needed. It serves the entire React application, REST API, WebSockets, and live reports from a single unified endpoint.

Rural Maharashtra Operating Environment & User Personas
Cognitive Ergonomics
🩺 Primary User Personas & Literacy Levels
  • ASHA Workers & ANMs (Auxiliary Nurse Midwives): Village-level and Sub-Centre staff. Primary language is Marathi. Often operate on low-cost Android phones with low keyboard typing comfort; require 1-tap voice dictation and visual iconography.
  • PHC Pharmacists: Responsible for daily medicine registers (दैनिक औषध नोंदवही) and stock audits. Highly familiar with physical paper registers; benefit from Gemini Vision OCR intake.
  • Taluka & District Health Officers (THO / DHO): Mobile decision makers monitoring emergency stockouts, monsoon road blockages, and inter-facility transfers.
🏔️ Geographical & Physical Realities (Sahyadri Range)
  • Monsoon Flooding & Cut-Off PHCs: In Bhor, Mahabaleshwar, and Velhe, torrential rains regularly isolate clinics for 24-72 hours. Rebalancing must account for ghat transit corridors.
  • Intermittent 2G/3G Connectivity: Network dropouts are routine. The UI must support optimistic local queueing and non-blocking offline operation.
  • Clinical Criticality: Stockouts of Anti-Snake Venom (ASV), Oxytocin, and Rabies Vaccines are fatal within 2-4 hours. Interface alerts cannot rely on subtle text labels alone.

🏛️ Architectural Alignment: DHIS2 Design System & Atomic Minimalism

PranaVahini adopts the proven principles of the DHIS2 Mobile Design System and Brad Frost's Atomic Design: (1) Pictorial Workflows: Complex medical operations are driven by bold, standardized iconography instead of text-dense instructions; (2) Progressive Disclosure: The frontline staff view displays only the immediate action (dispensing or challan capture), tucking 192-block cryptographic DSCSA ledgers and AI invariant math behind slide-over drawers; (3) Zero-Panic Offline Buffering: All transactions function deterministically offline, syncing silently upon 2G restoration.
